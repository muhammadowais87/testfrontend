The Spirit of Ramadan
1.	Ramadan celebrations in Pakistan are spectacular. People host huge dinners for the poor and needy. These are set up on the road sides where anyone can join in. All are welcome to these dinners and given food. With the large amounts of food present for anyone to eat, the generosity is clear. This custom is a display of the best characteristics of humankind.
2.	Islam is a religion of peace and tranquillity. It teaches its followers to be kind, dutiful and live in harmony with their neighbours, regardless of their caste or religion and during Ramadan by following the practices of Hazrat Muhammad, the world gets to see the true Islamic teachings.
3.	Celebrating Muslim festivals, most prominently Ramadan, has become a symbol for harmony and peace for the internation community. Though the spirit remains the same, each country has small differences in how they celebrate. In many countries, there are official Iftar parties hosted for their Muslim community. Non-Muslims are invited too so that the different communities can get to know one another and learn more about each other. These occasions show the world that Muslims and non-Muslims can live in peace and harmony.
Shandur Polo Festival
4.	Every year, from the 7th to the 9th of July, Shandur hosts a famous polo festival. The highlights of the festival are the polo matches between teams from Chitral and Gilgit. The two teams have been meeting here to compete since the pitch was built in 1936. However, polo has been a part of the local culture for much longer.
5.	The road between them winds along the mountain sides for nearly 400 km. Near the border between these two regions, almost halfway along the road, is a place called Shandur. Shandur is around 3738 metres above sea level and is surrounded by beautiful snow-capped mountains. During the summer months there is green grass on the plateau and the blue skies are reflected in Shandur lake.
6.	Polo is a fast-paced game. It has been called hockey on horses and it might be the world's oldest team sport. It has been played for over 2000 years. Polo is now played in over 100 countries and there are 16 professional polo teams in the world. Polo was an official Olympic sport from 1900 to 1939 and is now again recognised as one. The World Polo Championship, organised by the Federation of International Polo, is held every three years.
7.	As well as changes to the rules the equipment used has changed. On some ancient pitches the goals were made of stone but today most are made of vinyl and foam to prevent injury if they are crashed into. These days, the ball is made of plastic or wood, but in the past, it used to be made of willow root or hard rubber. Some say that long ago, other things such as the heads of animals or enemies, were used as a ball.
