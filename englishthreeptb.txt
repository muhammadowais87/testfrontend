All Are Welcome
1.	"Hello everyone! Today is my first day in class III. I am very happy. All my friends are also very happy. My name is Ali. I like to speak with my friends in English.
2.	My teachers halp me to learn through games and activities. I know some poems and I love to sing these with my friends. I come to school everyday to learn new things.
3.	It's fun to learn and play in school. There are boys and girls in my classroom. We listen to our teachers. There are a few rules for the classroom. My teacher has made a poster showing classroom rules so that we could follow them.
4.	A very important rule is to keep our classroom clean. The things we need are: brush, a mop, a water bucket, a duster, liquid soap and a sign board to show if the floor is wet. This habit will help us to keep our school and home clean.
The People I Love
5.	My name is Bano. I live in a small house. I call it a ‘Happy Home’. I have one elder sister and a brother who is younger to me. My parents take good care of us. My grandparents also live with us.
6.	We all feel very happy with each other. Our uncles, aunts and cousins come and meet us. We also go and visit them on the weekends or during vacation. It is all fun.
7.	My house has three rooms, a kitchen and a family room. There is a small garden in front of the house. I love to water the plants in the evening. I also keep a white kitten as a pet. I call it ‘kitty’.
8.	My grandfather tells me that a happy family home supports children and helps them to grow in their abilities and skills. It is also very important to listen to our parents and grandparents when they tell us important thing.
Kindness to Children
9.	Hazrat Muhammad ? is the last Rasool?. He? is a blessing for mankind. His life is a perfect example for everyone. His kindness to children is well known. The children also loved him very much.
10.	Hazrat Muhammad ? loved children. He? taught us how to treat them well and express our love and joy for them. A companion quoted Hazrat Anas? as saying, “I have never seen anyone act more kindly towards children than Allah’s Rasool”
11.	Once he? was giving the Friday sermon and he? saw his grand children Hazrat Imam Hassan? and Hazrat Imam Hussain? coming towards him. Their shirts were too long and they fell. He? got up and rushed to pick them up.
12.	When anyone brought the first fruit of the season, he? would give it to the youngest child present there. Whenever he? came back from journey, he? would let the children ride with him.
13.	His love and kindness was not confined to Muslim children only. Even in a battle, he? had ordered not to kill women and children. He? forgave the little mistakes the children made.
Road Safety
14.	All human activities start in the morning. People rush to their work places. Children are in a hurry to reach their schools. Therefore, we have to be very careful and watch around to be safe.
15.	Roads are made to help us go from one place to another. People, cycles, motorcycles, cars, truck and other heavy vehicles use roads day and night. After school, we find children running carelessly to cross the roads.
16.	It may cause accidents. In villages, animals come out and block the roads. It is, therefore, very important for everyone to follow rules so that we are safe. We see traffic police controlling hundreds of vehicles on the roads.
The Day of Silence
17.	Rizwan lived in a small village. His parents loved him dearly. He actively took part in sports. Everyone in the village liked him for his good manners. Rizwan could not hear since birth. When he turned three, a doctor advised his parents to help Rizwan learn sign language.
18.	Thus, Rizwan learnt sign language in a few weeks. Now, he was very happy because he was able to communicate with his parents. His parents took extra care of their only child. They always held on to his hand whenever they went out.
19.	Other villagers also treated him as a special child. This made Rizwan feel unhappy. His close friend Mumtaz noticed Rizwan’s unrest. He decided to set the things right. He requested his father to arrange a festival in honour of people who could not hear.
20.	During that whole day, everyone in the town would have to wear earplugs. People liked the idea. The day became known as ‘The Day of Silence’. When it arrived everyone stuck plugs in their ears.
I Like to Play
21.	Sports and games are full of fun for everyone. Children make friends for playing different games. These develop good health and sense of sharing. They learn to participate and work in groups. This helps them to know the people around them.
22.	There are games played in the ground like cricket, hockey, kabbadi, etc. Which are called outdoor games. There are indoor games as well like ludo, carom, etc. Which we play with our parents and siblings. Now-a-days, we also play many video and computer games.
23.	Through family games, a home becomes more cheerful. Children learn a lot when they play with their elders. In our society, it is very important for our children to play. There are many grounds near homes to play.
24.	there are many grounds near homes where children gather after school in the evening to play local games. Girls and boys run around to play the games they like. Girls enjoy swings more whereas the boys love to go round and round with the hanging tyre.
Saving Resources
25.	Nature has provided us many gifts such as air, water, land, sunlight, minerals, plants and animals. We need to conserve these resources. Existence on earth would not have been possible without any of these. These should be available to all in abundance.
26.	All these natural resources make life worth living on Earth. We cannot imagine life without any of these. Many of the resources are being used faster than they can be replaced. The use for these has increased because of over population.
27.	Humans, animals and plants, all need water to survive. It is significant for our daily life. It has numerous uses. We all must make every effort to use water carefully to avoid wastage. If we continue wasting water, there will be acute shortage of it in the near future.
28.	Switch off the extra lights and fans to save electricity. Switch over to energy saving methods and LEDs. These days solar energy is another method that can help us in saving electricity. Here are a few points to follow for saving electricity.
29.	Paper is made of trees. Increased use of paper means more trees will be cut down. Cutting down trees pollutes the environment. We should use only as much paper as is needed. We all must spread awareness about the conservation of natural resources.
My Culture-My Pride
30.	Our home Pakistan is a land of different cultures. From Khyber to Mehran, we all live happily with each other. A number of festivals and events are celebrated throughout the year. Pakistanis are hospitable people.
31.	We always take care of our guests. We cook many delicious dishes and invite our relatives. We wear colourful dresses. Local games and other events are arranged in which the young and the old take an active part to spend memorable time.
32.	Culture is the way we live, food we eat, dresses we wear, etc. The religion we follow reflects in our manners and routine. In Pakistani culture, we help needy people to solve their problems.
33.	Our children show respect to elders and they in return love the children. A senior citizen in a village or a city is respected by all in the neighbourhood as a close relation of theirs. We live as a family with our parents and grandparents. We are proud of our culture.
Healthy Habits
34.	A long time ago, there was a wizard who made a new spell. Whoever used the spell would get perfect teeth. The wizard decided to use it on one of his toads. After the spell was cast, the toad became a very smiley happy creature.
35.	Not only could he now eat all sorts of foods, but also he started speaking. “I’m delighted with the change”, repeated the toad, proudly, “I prefer eating candies to feeding on flies”.
36.	However, the toad didn’t pay much attention. He thought his teeth were too strong for him. He liked and enjoyed sweets so much that he didn’t even try to eat less. One day, a tooth started decaying and slowly the decay spread throughout his mouth.
37.	Poor Mr. Toad! If he hadn’t lost that last tooth he could have told the wizard to give him new teeth and would promise to take care of the new ones better. Nothing could be done now. There’s nothing more distasteful than eating bugs.
