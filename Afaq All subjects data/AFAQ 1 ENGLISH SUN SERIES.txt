Let's Meet Maida
1.	Maida was very excited. It was first day in her new class. She quickly got ready for school. Her father dropped her to school.
2.	She went to her new class and quietly sat on a desk. Miss Hadia entered the class. She welcomed all the students. "Please come one by one and tell everyone about yourself and your family". She said.
3.	" I am Maida. I am seven years old. I have a brother and two sisters. My mother is a doctor. My father is a businessman.
4.	I love keeping pets. My favourite subject is English. I have many storybook," Maida said. "That's really nice! Said Miss Hadia with a smile.
A Family Day Out
5.	It is Sunday. Hafsa and Zain are on holiday. They are very excited. They are going to the park for a picnic with their father and mother.
6.	Mrs. Ahmad is putting fruits and other things in the picnic basket. Hafsa and Zain get into the car with their toys. Soon they reach the park.
7.	Zain and Hafsa help their father to take out their things. They bring a sheet to sit on. The park has tall trees and green grass.
8.	Zain is playing football with his father. Mother is sitting and reading a book. Hafsa is collecting dry leaves. She sees some colourful butterflies on the flowers.
9.	She runs to catch one, but it fly away. She finds some insects in the ground. Today she explores that the soil is home to many crawlies.
10.	Hafsa wants to play on the swings. Her father takes her there. She is swinging back and forth. Zain wants to go to boating with his father.
Hazrat Muhammad (SAW)
11.	Our beloved Rasool Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) was known for his honesty. He (SAW) started trading at an early age with his uncle.
12.	People used to keep their things and money with him. Even his enemies always trusted his honesty. The people of Makkah gave him the title of Al-sadiq (SAW) (the truthful) and Al-Amin (SAW) (the trustworthy).
13.	When he (SAW) decided to move to Madinah, he(SAW) had money and things of people with him. He(SAW) asked Hazrat Ali (R.A) to stay in Makkah and return those things and money to the owners.
14.	Hazrat Ali (R.A) stayed in Makkah until he had returned everything to everyone. Rasoolullah (SAW) taught us to be honest and truthful.
Save Water, Save life!
15.	Basit and Anaya are cousine. Basit is washing his bicycle in the garage. Anaya enters his house.
16.	Yes i know. But you have left the water tap open. Use a basket or close the tap when you don't need water. We need to save water so that our lakes and rivers don't run dry too quickly.
17.	Oh! I didn't realise that. I am sorry. I will try my best to save water now. Anaya: yes we all should save water. We need water for our needs.
A Fun Day!
18.	Edi-ul.Fitar is a special event for Muslims all over the world. They celebrate Eid-Ul-Fitar in the month of Shawaal. It is also called 'Chotti Eid'
19.	Last year, we went to spend our Eid holidays with my grandparents in Sialkot. We celebrated Eid with them. I woke up early in the morning and got ready for Eid prayer.
20.	After Eid prayer, we ate the sweet dish my mother had made for us. After that, my grandfather went to a child welfare centre near us to give Eidi and gifts to the children there.
21.	I Also went with him. The children were very happy to get gifts. My grandfather told me that it was our duty to share our joys with them. Allah likes this.
The Foolish crow
22.	Blakie was a crow who lived in the forest with other birds. He would look at the birds around him and say:" How beautiful all these birds are! I am so black and ugly.
23.	Blakie was a crow who lived in the forest with other birds. He would look at the birds around him and say:" How beautiful all these birds are! I am so black and ugly.
24.	One day all the birds decided to choose the most beautiful bird amongst them. Sippy, the sparrow was the judge was the for the competition.
25.	He flew over the nest of each and every bird in the forest and secretly collected their feathers. He collected many different colourful feathers. Finally, he flew over to his nest and put all the feathers onto his body.
26.	Sippy was surprised as she had never seen such a bird in the forest before. Blakie proudly started walking and flying around to show off his colourful feather.
27.	Soon they all understood that it was Blakie who wanted to win the competition. They all laughed at him. Blakie realised that he had made fun of himself so he apologised to his friends.
We are Alive!
28.	It was Sunday, Hashim woke up early in the morning. He got up from bed and went to see his plants in the lawn. There he saw a beautiful sunflower on a plant.
29.	"Wow! what a lovely flower we have today in our lawn" he said to himself. He sat near the plant looking at the bloom." How beautiful you are! "said Hashim.
30.	We eat and grow. I can change the direction with the Sun. explained the sunflower. "Wow! I never knew that. I always thought that only human beings were alive. You are amazing.
