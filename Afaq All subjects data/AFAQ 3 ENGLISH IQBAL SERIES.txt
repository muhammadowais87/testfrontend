3.	With this thought in mind, I went to sleep. Suddenly, I found myself in a place all covered with snow. It was really freezing there.
4.	Who are you? I asker. " My name is Pingo and I am a penguin, he replied. "Where am I?" I asked in surprise. "You are on my iceberg, " Pingo replied. "Who are you?" "My name is Umair. I am from a far-off land, "I replied.
5.	Within a few minutes, we became friend. Pingo introduced me to his large family. "We live together in families. we like to eat fish. We have a strong sense of hearing and sight, which helps us in catching fish," explained Pingo.
