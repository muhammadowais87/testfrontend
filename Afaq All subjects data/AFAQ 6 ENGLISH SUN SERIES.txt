1.1 Seerat-un-Nabi (رسول اللہ خاتم النبینﷺ)
1.	Allah (سُبَِْحانَه وَتَعَالٰى) sent many messengers and prophets to this world, but Hazrat Muhammad (رسول اللہ خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) is the last messenger and Prophet of Allah (سُبَِْحانَه وَتَعَالٰى). He ( خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) was a great teacher and reformer. His character and personality server as the best model for the whole mankind.
2.	He ( خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) spent his whole life portraying honesty, truthfulness, bravery and noble conduct. He ( خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) always stood fair and honest in his dealings. Even before Allah (سُبَِْحانَه وَتَعَالٰى) sent the first revelation to him, he ( خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) was known as Al-Sadiq (the truthful) and Al-Amin (the trustworthy).
3.	In his young age, he ( خَاتَمُ النَّبِیّٰن ﷺ) was a successful but honest trader.
