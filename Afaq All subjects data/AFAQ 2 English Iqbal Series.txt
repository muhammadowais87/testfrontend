My Beloved Pakistan
1.	Pakistan is my dear homeland. My country is very beautiful. It has high mountains, green grassy plains and large deserts. The capital city of Pakistan is Islamabad.
2.	Our flag has two colours: green and white. The people of Pakistan are very loving, Kind and brave. Pakistani children are hard-working and intelligent.
3.	My country has a rich cultural heritage. It consists of different cultural groups such as Punjabis, Sindhis, Balochis, Pakhtuns, Kasmiris and Gilgit-Baltistanis.
Mother's Love
4.	A mother duck and her ducklings lived near a small pond. Once, the mother duck went to a lake with her ducklings. The ducklings were very happy to see so much water.
5.	The mother duck began to walk back and acted as if her wing was broken. When the fox saw the mother duck, he thought that the duck was hurt and could not fly. So, he ran to eat her.
6.	The mother duck started to run and the fox followed her. By that time, the ducklings had reached the lake. The fox was not able to harm the ducklings.
7.	Then the mother duck opened her wings and quickly rose up in the air. She landed in the middle of the lake. All the ducklings swam to her. The fox looked at the mother duck and her ducklings greedily.
Sami's First day at School
8.	It was Sami's first day at school. During break time, all students went to the playground. Everyone started playing except Sami.He sat quietly on a bench.
9.	He did not talk to anyone. Miss Sara noticed that he was upset and not talking to anyone. She came to him.
10.	Miss Sara called some of the students from his class and asked them to take Sami to the playground and play with him. The students went to the playground.
The Greatest Message of Equality
11.	Once Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) and his companions were going somewhere. They had lambs and other animals with them. After covering a short distance, they felt tired and hungry.
12.	The companions divided different tasks among themselves Rasoolullah (SAW) said, " I will collect wood to make a fire." The companions said that Rasoolullah (SAW) you should not worry about it.
13.	We will do everything by ourselves. Rasoolullah (SAW) replied," I know you all can manage it very well. But the person who does not do anything and lest others work for him cannot please Allah.
In the Park
14.	There is a park near my house. My friends and i go to the park daily at 5 o'clock in the evening. We play there with other children.
15.	There are manu colourful plants and flowers in the park. Tall green trees are everywhere in the park. beautiful birds chirps in these trees.
16.	The gardner water the plants daily. We make sure not to pluck leaves and flowers. There is also a small pond in the park. Many fish and ducks live in it.
17.	We do not throw wrappers in it. There are two large dustbins placed in the park. Every week, the gardener takes out twings and straw and puts them in bins.
Eid-ul-Fitar
18.	Eid is a special event for Muslims all over the world. They celebrate Eid-ul-Fitar in Shawaal. It is also called 'Chooti Eid'. Eid is a gift of Allah for Muslims. They fast during the whole month of Ramadan, the ninth month of the Islamic calendar and then celebrate Eid-ul-Fitar.
19.	Muslims help the poor to make them part of their Eid celebration by giving away Fitr, clothes and gifts. People wear new dresses on Eid dya. They go to the masjid to say Eid prayer.
20.	The embrace ans wish each other Eid Mubarak. On Eid Day, friends and relatives visit each other. They eat, spend time together and also exchange gifts.
21.	Last year, i celebrated Eid in Sialkot with my grandparents, uncles and aunts. We woke up early in the morning to say our Fajr prayer. Then we recited the Holy Quran. Next we wore new dresses.
22.	I put on a shalwar kameez and my younger sister Aisha wore a colourful dress. She also put on bangles and jewellery. I went to say Eid prayer with my grandfather. My mother and aunts cooked special dishes for all the family members.
An Ant and a Dove
23.	On a hot summer day, an ant was thirsty. But she could not find water to drink. She walked around for hours and reached a river. She climbed up on a stone to drink water.
24.	There was a dove sitting in a tree, who saw the ant fall into the river. The dove quickly plucked a leaf and dropped it near the ant. The ant onto the leaf.
25.	Soon, the leaf moved slowly and reached the bank of the river. The ant jumped onto the dry ground. She thanked the dove. They became friends. The next day, a bird catcher came there He planned to trap the dove with his net.
26.	The ant and saw him and thought about what the bird catches would do. The dove was sleeping and she did not know about the bird catcher. The ant quickly bit hime on the foot.
