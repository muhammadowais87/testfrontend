4.	They were far away far away from the their home. soon, it started raining. They waited for the rain to stop.
5.	After some time, the rain stopped. They flew back to the forest. As they reached there, the mother finch found her nest broken.

My First Painting
11.	Sahar is my younger sister. She loves making greeting cards. Today i am going to tell her how to make a greeting card with the help of leaf printing.
12.	First take a piece of chart paper and fold it in half. Then, apply any watercolour on the leaf with a painbrush.
13.	After that, put the coloured side of the leaf on one half of the chart paper in different directions and press it well.
14.	Finally, write your greetings in the chart paper. you can also write your phone number with your name if you are inviting your friend.
15.	The card is ready. Now, you can give it to your friend or cousin, says Fiza she asks her sister to clean the room, wash her hands and put things in place. Sahar thanks her for the help.
Two Friends
16.	Hafsa and Zoha are friends. They live next to each other. Hafsa always shares her toys with Zoha but Zoha never shares her things with her. Once Zoha's father bought her a fluffy grey rabbit. It was really cute. Zoha was very happy.
17.	She used to play with it in the park every evening. Hafsa also wanted to play with her rabbit. But Zoha never invited her to play with it.
18.	One evening, Zoha forgot her rabbit in the park. She looked for it everywhere at home but could not find it. She was really sad and upset.
19.	Hafsa told her to look for it in the park. They both went to the park. There, they found the rabbit in a bin. When Zoha returned home, her mother said " see, how Hafsa helped you.
20.	She shares everything with you. You should also be nice to her. Sharing is always good " Zoha had realised her mistake. She thanked Hafsa for helping her.
